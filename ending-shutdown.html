Shutdown Ending

1,743 MW of design load vanishes off the grid, immediately easing blackout risks in Dublin SouthWest and relieving local grid transformers. Disconnecting facilities jeopardizes €2.2 Billion in total Gross Value Added (GVA) and puts 19,500 direct, indirect, and induced jobs at risk. National tax revenue loses €280 Million in employment-related taxes. Essential cloud services, hospital data syncs, and financial sector systems experience severe disruption.
